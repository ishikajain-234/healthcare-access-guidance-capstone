# Safety Boundary Specification

**Project:** Healthcare Access Guidance Prototype  
**Classification:** Academic Demonstration Prototype (2nd-Year AP + Data Engineering Capstone)  
**Safety Status:** Non-Clinical, Non-Eligibility, Non-Diagnostic Administrative Guidance  

---

## 1. What the Prototype Does
* Provides general, simulated information routing using a controlled demonstration dropdown of fictitious service categories.
* Initializes and manages anonymous guidance sessions using ephemeral UUIDv4 identifiers.
* Enforces a deterministic 7-state administrative guidance state machine.
* Displays synthetic reference cards showing simulated inclusions and exclusions.
* Intercepts "no-match" selections to demonstrate an automated recovery workflow.
* Displays static, official public information/help channels (e.g., placeholder for national helplines and beneficiary portals).
* Persists append-only, anonymous state transition events for analytical telemetry.
* Executes a Python ETL script to compute operational metrics from session event data.

---

## 2. What the Prototype Does NOT Do (Strict Prohibitions)
1. **No Medical Decisions:**
   * Does NOT interpret symptoms, diagnose medical conditions, triage urgency, or advise on health conditions.
   * Does NOT recommend medical treatments, procedures, doctors, clinical departments, or hospitals.
2. **No Scheme Eligibility or Coverage Adjudication:**
   * Does NOT check, verify, determine, or confirm individual insurance or government scheme eligibility.
   * Does NOT guarantee cashless treatment, pre-authorization, coverage approval, or financial reimbursement.
   * Does NOT evaluate patient billing or out-of-pocket costs.
3. **No Operational Hospital Claims:**
   * Does NOT assert or simulate live hospital bed occupancy, doctor on-duty rotas, appointment slots, or live queue lengths.
4. **No Identity or Health Record Collection:**
   * Does NOT collect, store, or process Aadhaar numbers, ABHA addresses, scheme-card identifiers, names, phone numbers, addresses, or dates of birth.
   * Does NOT ingest medical reports, prescriptions, clinical notes, or file uploads.
5. **No Grievance or Dispute Adjudication:**
   * Does NOT accept, submit, process, or evaluate complaints or fraud allegations against any hospital.
6. **No Live Third-Party Integration:**
   * Does NOT connect to PM-JAY, NHA, ABDM, ORS, eHospital, or hospital HMIS databases.

---

## 3. Synthetic Seed Data Nature
* All demonstration records are entirely fictional and generated by the student capstone team.
* Seed records do not contain package prices, official scheme rates, or government URLs.
* Every record is explicitly flagged with `is_simulated_reference = true` and `data_status = 'SYNTHETIC_DEMO'`.

---

## 4. Mandatory Conditional Language
All user interfaces and API responses strictly enforce conditional language:
* *“This is a student prototype using simulated demonstration data...”*
* *“Public reference information suggests...”*
* *“Official verification through an authorized channel is required...”*
* *“This tool does not confirm eligibility, coverage, approval, hospital availability, or cashless treatment.”*
